Rules and settings

  • Infinite deck: every card is drawn from a full 52-card deck, so card counting does not work here. For counting, use the Blackjack Table Simulator (real 8-deck shoe).
  • Dealer stands on all 17s and checks for blackjack with an ace or a ten up.
  • Blackjack pays 3:2, insurance pays 2:1.
  • Double on any first two cards, double after split allowed. One split per hand; split aces get one card each.
  • No surrender.
  • Perfect Pairs (your first two cards): mixed pair 6:1, coloured pair 12:1, perfect pair 25:1.
  • 21+3 (your two cards and the dealer up card): flush 5:1, straight 10:1, three of a kind 30:1, straight flush 40:1, suited trips 100:1.
  • Side bets cost far more than the main bet in the long run; play them for fun, not as strategy.
  • Hotkeys: Space bet, H hit, S stand, D double, P split, Y/N insurance.
  • Play money only. Balance and history are saved to your account when this page can reach it.
