Other players use basic strategy. Player 1 bets reverse martingale: €10 to start, double after a win, back to €10 after a loss, same bet after a push; a double that would pass the €500 table limit goes back to €10. Their profit and loss shows under their names; reset it with the button above (a bankroll reset clears it too). Rules: blackjack pays 3:2. The dealer checks for blackjack only with an ace up, after offering insurance. With a ten up there is no check: a dealer blackjack shows at the end and takes only your original bet (double and split money is returned). Double after split, late surrender if allowed, split up to 4 hands, one card to split aces.

The shoe holds real decks (8 × 52 = 416 cards), shuffled with your browser's cryptographic random generator. The cut card goes in the middle (45–55% of the shoe left behind) or deep (20–25% left) as you choose, a little different every shoe; the shoe shows how far away it is. When it comes out the hand is finished, then the whole shoe is reshuffled. Between hands you can always ask for a new shoe.
