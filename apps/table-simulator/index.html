Other players use basic strategy. Player 1 bets reverse martingale: €10 to start, double after a win, back to €10 after a loss, same bet after a push; a double that would pass the €500 table limit goes back to €10. Their profit and loss shows under their names; reset it with the button above (a bankroll reset clears it too). Rules: blackjack pays 3:2. The dealer's second card is dealt face down. With an ace up the dealer offers insurance (2:1) and then checks for blackjack. With a ten up there is no check: a dealer blackjack shows at the end and takes every bet, double and split money too. Double on any two cards, also after a split. Split a pair once; split aces get one card each. Perfect Pairs pays 25:1 perfect, 12:1 coloured, 6:1 mixed; 21+3 pays 100:1 suited trips, 40:1 straight flush, 30:1 three of a kind, 10:1 straight, 5:1 flush. Because of the unchecked ten the chart here hits 11 against a ten and does not split 8,8 against a ten.

The shoe holds real decks (8 × 52 = 416 cards), shuffled with your browser's cryptographic random generator. The cut card goes in the middle (45–55% of the shoe left behind) or deep (20–25% left) as you choose, a little different every shoe; the shoe shows how far away it is. When it comes out the hand is finished, then the whole shoe is reshuffled. Between hands you can always ask for a new shoe.
